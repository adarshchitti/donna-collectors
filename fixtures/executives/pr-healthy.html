Nordvale Materials Names Jane Doe Chief Financial Officer

Feb 1, 2024

COLUMBUS, Ohio, Feb. 1, 2024 /PRNewswire/ -- Nordvale Materials Inc. today announced the appointment of Jane Doe as Chief Financial Officer, effective February 1, 2024. Doe succeeds interim CFO Alan Pryce.

Doe joins Nordvale from Harland Supply Co., where she served as treasurer.
