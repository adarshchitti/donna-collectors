Bath & Body Works Appoints Daniel Heaf as Chief Executive Officer

May 19, 2025

COLUMBUS, Ohio, May 19, 2025 -- Bath & Body Works, Inc. (NYSE: BBWI) today announced that its Board of Directors has appointed Daniel Heaf as Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board, effective immediately. Heaf succeeds Gina Boswell, who has stepped down from her role as CEO and a member of the Board.

Heaf most recently served as Nike's chief strategy and transformation officer.
